### Drop Stitch Technology for a Premium Appearance

For this project, we explored drop stitch construction as the foundation of the product design.

Drop stitch technology uses thousands of internal connecting threads to connect two fabric layers, creating a relatively firm and structured shape when inflated to the specified pressure.

Compared with conventional low-pressure inflatable products, drop stitch structures can provide a more defined, clean and premium appearance.

This makes them an interesting option for:

- Premium brand activations.
- Outdoor product displays.
- Exhibition installations.
- Promotional swimming pool concepts.
- Customized event environments.

The structure, dimensions and inflation specifications are developed according to the intended application.

## 4. More Than Manufacturing: Our ODM Development Process

For advertising agencies and event marketing companies, product development needs to be flexible.

The final product often starts with an idea rather than an engineering drawing.

Our ODM process is designed to bridge that gap.

**Step 1 — Creative Concept Review.** The customer shares campaign references, brand guidelines, sketches, dimensions or inspiration images. We evaluate the proposed application and identify the main product requirements.

**Step 2 — Product Design & Structure Development.** Our team develops the product configuration, dimensions, material options and drop stitch construction according to the project requirements.

**Step 3 — Branding & Graphic Customization.** We work with the customer to develop the product's visual appearance, including brand colors, logo placement and printed graphics.

**Step 4 — Prototype & Sample Development.** A sample can be developed to evaluate appearance, dimensions, construction and functional requirements before production.

**Step 5 — Product Testing & Quality Inspection.** Depending on the intended use, we can evaluate material performance, welding strength, air leakage, water containment and structural stability.

**Step 6 — Customized Manufacturing & Delivery.** After design approval, we proceed with the agreed production specifications, packaging and delivery arrangements.
