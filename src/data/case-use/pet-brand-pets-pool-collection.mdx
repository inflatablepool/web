## 1. A New Opportunity in the Growing Pet Products Market

The global pet industry is evolving.

Today's pet owners are no longer satisfied with basic pet accessories. They are increasingly looking for specialized products that improve their pets' comfort, safety and quality of life.

Among these opportunities, pet swimming pools are becoming an interesting product category, especially for families with dogs and outdoor living spaces.

However, a conventional inflatable swimming pool is not necessarily suitable for pets.

Dogs have different physical characteristics and usage habits compared with humans.

Their claws can scratch surfaces. Their movements can create concentrated pressure on pool walls and floors. Jumping, turning, digging and playing can place additional stress on product materials and seams.

A pet pool therefore needs more than an attractive appearance.

It needs to be developed around the real characteristics of pets.

One of our pet brand customers recognized this opportunity and approached Inflatable Pool Factory with an ambitious idea:

**To develop a dedicated Pets Pool product collection under its own brand.**

Instead of simply putting a pet logo on an existing swimming pool, the customer wanted a product series specifically designed for dogs and other household pets.

This became the starting point of our OEM/ODM product development project.

## 2. Understanding Pets Before Designing the Product

Our product development team believes that successful pet products begin with understanding how animals actually use them.

Before discussing product appearance and specifications, we focused on several important characteristics of dogs.

### Strong Claws and Scratching Behavior

Unlike human users, dogs naturally use their paws and claws to interact with their surroundings.

They may scratch the pool floor when entering, exiting or playing in the water.

Some dogs also have a habit of digging or pushing against surfaces.

This creates a specific challenge for inflatable pool construction.

**Our development direction:**

- Explore more abrasion-resistant PVC surface materials.
- Evaluate reinforced fabric and coating options.
- Consider additional protection in high-contact areas.
- Improve the surface construction to reduce the risk of damage from normal pet activities.
- Evaluate material performance through abrasion and puncture testing.

Our objective is to develop a pool surface better suited to pet use, rather than simply using standard recreational pool materials.

### Active Movements and Concentrated Pressure

Dogs rarely remain still in a swimming pool.

They move around, turn quickly, jump in and out, and sometimes push their bodies against the pool walls.

These movements create repeated mechanical stress.

**Our development direction:**

- Evaluate the structural strength of the drop stitch construction.
- Optimize the wall and floor configuration.
- Review reinforcement requirements in high-stress areas.
- Test the product under representative loading and movement conditions.
- Improve the overall structural design according to the intended product size and application.

The goal is to create a more stable and durable product for everyday pet recreation.

### Sharp Claws and Puncture Risks

One of the most important differences between human swimming pools and dog swimming pools is the possibility of puncture damage caused by sharp claws.

A small puncture can affect the performance of an inflatable product.

For this reason, we pay special attention to the PVC surface, material layers and protective construction.

**Our development direction:**

- Evaluate higher-performance PVC material options.
- Explore additional protective surface layers.
- Assess resistance to puncture and tearing.
- Consider reinforced bottom and wall construction.
- Conduct puncture resistance tests on selected material combinations.

We aim to make the product more resistant to the challenges associated with pet use.

### Water, Moisture and Repeated Cleaning

Pet swimming pools may require more frequent cleaning than ordinary family pools.

Hair, dirt, sand and outdoor debris can accumulate during use.

**Our development direction:**

- Select PVC materials suitable for repeated water exposure and cleaning.
- Optimize surface finishing for easier maintenance.
- Review seam sealing and bonding quality.
- Consider convenient drainage solutions.
- Design for practical deflation, drying and storage.

A good pet pool should be convenient not only for pets, but also for the people who take care of them.

## 3. Five Key Areas of Our Pet Pool Product Development

Based on the customer's requirements, we organized the development project around five important product engineering areas.

### 1. Pet-Friendly PVC Surface Development

The PVC surface is one of the most important components of a pet pool.

We can explore different PVC formulations, thicknesses, reinforcement fabrics and surface finishes according to the target market and product positioning.

Development considerations include:

- Abrasion resistance.
- Puncture resistance.
- Tear resistance.
- Surface durability.
- Water resistance.
- Cleaning and maintenance requirements.

Rather than simply selecting the thickest material, we evaluate the balance between durability, flexibility, product weight and manufacturing feasibility.

### 2. Reinforced Seams and Connection Areas

Seams are critical structural areas of an inflatable pool.

For pet applications, they may experience repeated stress from jumping, scratching and pushing.

Our development team can evaluate:

- PVC welding processes.
- Seam width and construction.
- Reinforced connection areas.
- Corner and edge configurations.
- Production consistency.
- Seam strength and leakage testing.

Our objective is to reduce the risk of seam failure and improve long-term product reliability.

### 3. A More Durable Pool Bottom

The pool bottom is exposed to two different types of pressure.

First, it supports the water and the weight of the animals.

Second, it receives direct contact from paws and claws during movement.

For this reason, bottom construction deserves special attention.

Our development options include:

- Abrasion-resistant bottom material.
- Additional protective layers.
- Reinforced fabric construction.
- Improved surface durability.
- Optional protective ground mats.

The exact configuration can be developed according to the intended dog size, pool dimensions and target retail positioning.

### 4. Drop Stitch Structural Development

Drop stitch technology provides a distinctive structural advantage for premium inflatable pools.

Thousands of internal connecting threads link the two fabric layers, allowing the structure to maintain a relatively flat and firm shape when inflated to the specified pressure.

For pet pool applications, we can evaluate:

- Drop stitch material specifications.
- Pool wall thickness and height.
- Overall structural configuration.
- Inflation pressure requirements.
- Wall and floor connection design.
- Product dimensions and load requirements.

The aim is to combine the clean appearance of a premium drop stitch pool with construction considerations specific to pet use.

### 5. Pet-Friendly Functional Design

A successful Pets Pool collection should consider the entire user experience.

Beyond the main pool structure, we can explore additional functional features:

- Convenient drainage outlets.
- Practical water inlet and outlet configurations.
- Easy-clean surfaces.
- Protective ground accessories.
- Deflation and storage solutions.
- Optional pet entry and exit accessories.
- Different sizes for different dog breeds.

Every feature is evaluated according to product feasibility, safety and the customer's target market.
