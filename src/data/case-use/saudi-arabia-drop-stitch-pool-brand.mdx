A young Saudi entrepreneur noticed this market opportunity.

He started looking for a more premium alternative.

That's when he discovered drop stitch inflatable pools.

Unlike conventional soft-wall inflatable pools, drop stitch technology uses thousands of connecting threads between two fabric layers. When inflated to the specified pressure, the structure becomes firm and maintains a relatively flat, rigid shape.

It offers an attractive combination of:

- A firm, structured pool design.
- A relatively flat and stable wall structure.
- Convenient deflation and storage.
- A premium appearance for modern outdoor spaces.
- Flexible applications for home leisure and recreation.
