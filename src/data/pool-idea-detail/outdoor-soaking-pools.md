## 3. Heater Compatibility & Temperature Limits

Creating a warm plunge or regulating summer pool temperatures requires a clear understanding of equipment compatibility and thermal safety thresholds.

- **Heater compatibility:**
  - Inflatable Pool Factory soaking pools are compatible with external inline electric heat pumps or solar pool heaters when connected via standard bypass hose fittings.
  - External heaters must be paired with continuous circulation filter pumps to ensure even heat distribution and prevent localized hotspots at the inlet.

- **Material & seam thermal limits:**
  - **Maximum recommended water temperature:** Up to **40°C (104°F)** under standard operating conditions.
  - **Ambient operating range:** Designed for optimal performance in ambient air temperatures ranging from **5°C to 45°C (41°F to 113°F)**.

> **Thermal safety & seasonal use notice:** Inflatable Pool Factory drop-stitch pools are engineered primarily for summer cooling and mild-weather thermal soaking up to 40°C (104°F). They are NOT rated or certified as dedicated year-round hot tubs or sub-zero winter spa systems.
>
> - **Do not use in freezing weather:** Operating or storing filled pools in sub-freezing temperatures (below 0°C / 32°F) risks structural seam degradation, material stiffness, and ice expansion damage.
> - **No extreme boiling water:** Never pour boiling water directly onto pool surfaces or seams.

## 5. Daily Care Checklist for Low-Effort Relaxation

Keeping your private soaking pool crystal clear requires minimal time when following a routine:

1. **Cover immediately after use:** Always secure a fitted thermo-reflective cover right after soaking to lock in temperature, cut evaporation, and keep out windborne dust or leaves.
2. **Run quiet filtration:** Set your cartridge filter pump on a timer for 2 to 4 hours daily during low-noise hours (such as early morning) to maintain water clarity without disrupting evening peace.
3. **Gentle sanitization:** Use a floating tablet dispenser with mild chlorine or bromine spa tablets to keep water pristine with minimal chemical odor.
