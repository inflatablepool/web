## 1. Space Planning & Actual Usable Dimensions

Proper spatial planning is the first step toward a seamless and enjoyable backyard pool experience.

- **Site selection principles:**
  - **Flat, level surface:** Choose a firm, level area completely free of sharp objects (rocks, twigs, or debris). We recommend using a heavy-duty ground cloth underneath the pool.
  - **Sunlight & privacy:** Position the pool in a spot that receives plenty of sunlight while being sheltered from harsh winds. Consider neighbor privacy and easy access to water lines and drainage.
  - **Clear clearance zone:** Maintain a clear pathway of at least 1 to 1.5 meters around all sides of the pool for easy entry, exit, lounging, and routine maintenance.

- **Actual usable space:**
  - Traditional soft-sided inflatable pools feature sloped inner walls that eat into your actual swimming area. Inflatable Pool Factory utilizes high-density [drop-stitch technology](/guides/what-is-a-drop-stitch-inflatable-pool), resulting in firm, upright, ultra-rigid side walls.
  - This means **the advertised dimensions closely match your actual usable space**. A medium-to-large rectangular drop-stitch pool delivers well over 90% usable interior water surface — giving multiple adults and children ample room to splash, stretch out, and play without feeling cramped.

## 2. Water Capacity & Multi-Person Usability

When hosting family gatherings, weight capacity and water volume are crucial considerations:

- **Capacity guidelines:**
  - **Small to medium family pools (2–3 people):** Hold approximately 1,000 to 2,000 liters, perfect for cozy parent-child play.
  - **Spacious family pools (4–6+ people or parties):** Hold 3,000 to 6,000+ liters. Thanks to the structural rigidity of the drop-stitch material, the side walls will not sag, buckle, or bulge even when filled to capacity.

- **Multi-person experience:**
  - Drop-stitch construction incorporates thousands of high-tensile polyester threads connecting the top and bottom layers, creating an exceptionally rigid structure when inflated to the rated operating pressure. Adults can comfortably lean against the pool edge without causing water overflow or structural collapse, making it ideal for multi-person summer gatherings.

## 3. Quick & Easy Setup Guide

Forget about costly yard excavation or complex construction. Transforming your backyard into a water park is fast and straightforward with Inflatable Pool Factory:

1. **Clear the ground:** Thoroughly sweep and inspect the area, clearing away any debris or sharp objects.
2. **Lay the ground cloth:** Unfold the protective ground cloth and lay the pool body flat over it.
3. **Rapid inflation:** Connect the provided high-pressure electric pump to inflate the walls and floor. In just 5 to 10 minutes, the pool inflates to a rigid, hard-sided standard.
4. **Fill with water:** Ensure all inflation valves and drain caps are securely closed, then begin filling. Gently smooth out any bottom folds as the pool fills.

## 5. Child Safety & Drowning Prevention (Critical)

While water activities are full of fun, safety remains the highest priority at all times.

> **Essential safety warning:** An inflatable pool is NOT a lifesaving or anti-drowning device. Regardless of water depth, drowning hazards exist. Children using the pool MUST be under continuous, close adult supervision ("touch supervision") at all times. Never leave a child unattended near or in the pool, even for a moment.

- **Safety best practices:**
  - **Constant active supervision:** Supervising adults should maintain direct visual contact at all times and avoid distractions (such as mobile phones, reading, or stepping away).
  - **Drain & store after use:** When the pool is not in active use or during extended periods, drain the water completely or restrict physical access (for example, using secure pool fencing or storing the pool away) to prevent children from wandering near unsupervised water.
  - **Slip prevention:** Instruct children not to run or jump on wet surfaces around the pool edges, and use non-slip mats at entry and exit points.
