## Round Blue Inflatable Drop Stitch Swimming Pool

The Inflatable Pool Factory round blue pool is the classic backyard swimming pool, rebuilt with modern drop stitch technology. The bold blue finish looks great on any patio or lawn, while the high-pressure drop stitch walls inflate firm and help the pool hold its round shape through the season.

## Key Features

- **Classic round design** — an even depth all around, ideal for family swimming and games.
- **Drop stitch rigidity** — walls inflate hard at 4–5 PSI and hold their shape under load.
- **Quick setup and takedown** — about 10 minutes to inflate; drains and folds flat for storage.
- **Vibrant blue UV-resistant surface** — color-fast, easy to clean, built for full summer seasons.
- **Grey colorway available** — the same round series in a modern grey finish, with an optional matching fitted cover.
- **Complete package** — includes electric air pump, repair kit, and storage bag.
