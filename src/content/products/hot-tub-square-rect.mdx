## Square and Rectangular Inflatable Hot Tub SPA Pool

The Inflatable Pool Factory square and rectangular constant temperature SPA tub turns a patio, conservatory, or private courtyard into a year-round wellness spot when paired with a compatible heating unit (sold separately). Rigid double-layer PVC drop stitch walls (15/20 cm profiles) hold their shape at the recommended 4–5 PSI operating pressure, while the built-in integrated inflatable seats and 5 mm insulated thermal bottom base lock in heat and adapt to heating units and spa therapy equipment.

Unlike seasonal-only inflatable pools, this series is engineered for year-round hot tub and hydrotherapy use — and still folds into compact carton packaging for storage or shipping.

## Key Features

- **Built-in integrated inflatable seats** — ergonomically formed seating for comfortable soaks.
- **5 mm insulated thermal bottom** — an insulated base layer that retains heat and cuts heating costs.
- **Rigid drop stitch walls** — 15/20 cm wall profiles stay flat and firm at 4–5 PSI (factory QC tested at 6 PSI).
- **Year-round use** — pairs with external circulation heaters (sold separately), sand filter pumps, inline disinfection, and air bubble massage systems.
- **Rapid setup and storage** — one person inflates the tub in 5–15 minutes with the 1000W electric pump; deflates and folds flat in the off-season.
